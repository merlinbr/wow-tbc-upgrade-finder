Design notes

Prototype boundary. Only tabs and these notes work. No credential is generated, shown, exchanged or stored; access buttons do not authenticate.

Protected exchange. Treat recovery links like passwords. Exchange credentials through a protected flow, never long-lived query-string secrets, public Share, analytics or ordinary request logs.

Failure state. Invalid or revoked recovery grants no access. Preserve entered work and show an error; never claim a successful restore.

Design notes

Confirm before changing access. Replacement and revocation require explicit confirmation and explain that the old guest credential and browser access using it will fail. Replacement must retain new access; no action is completed in this prototype.

Backups are separate. File export/import restores character data, not credentials or ownership. It cannot prove editing access to the original character.

Use an account instead

Optional account access lets you reopen your characters across devices.

Sign in, then attach Elara after proving guest editing access. Attachment keeps the same public URL and retires guest credentials.

Design notes

Ownership boundary. Account attachment requires proof of guest editing access and is atomic. It preserves the public URL and invalidates prior guest credentials; account ownership is not proof of in-game identity.

Prototype boundary. These buttons do not sign in or attach anything. Use a maintained authentication solution during implementation, not a custom password or account-recovery system.
